OTHER GAME IDEAS

Ideas that haven't left the drawing board. Yet.

IDEA // 01

ERSATZ

A 2.5D story-driven fighting game with an emphasis on string-based combos and fast-paced anime-fighter style movesets, featuring a nice set of unique characters each with their own distinct playstyles and game feel as well as fleshed out roles they serve in the overall narrative of the game.

IDEA // 02

Ping Pong Warriors

A stylized, fast-paced 3D sports game with gameplay mechanics primarily centered around the Table Tennis sport. An added feature is a tri-class system that enhances the player character's gameplay flow (speed, strength, guard).

02
IDEA // 03

Grapple God

Either a 2D or 3D competitive physics-based multiplayer game focusing on high octane energy and precise, skill-based gameplay mechanics. There needs not be a story. Just race to the top and become the Grapple God.

03
IDEA // 04

My Way Home

A 3D retro PSX ambient platformer that follows a Siamese cat's perspective as they traverse through the city in search of their owner. On the journey, the player will begin uncovering the cat's story and how they came to find themselves in the custody of their owner, and the struggles both of them had to endure before and during each other's company.

04
IDEA // 05

KHIMERRA

A 3D action-adventure hack and slash game where the primary mechanic is stance-switching. To be more accurate, trait-switching. These traits are from supernatural animals that can either grant you more aggressive attacks, strengthen your defensive capabilities, give you more awareness of the battlefield, offer you a cunning edge by providing access to tricks, or near omniscient wisdom in combat.

Extra: Later on in the game, you are given one last trait which is considered to be your magnum opus or ultimate trait. It grants you the ability to completely negate all damage dealt to you, with the cost of someone else in particular taking the damage.

IDEA // 06

Echoes of the Empyrean

A fully 3D, stylized, open-world RPG experience that focuses on immersive gameplay, a complex magic + fighting combat system, intuitive ship battle mechanics, and most importantly, a compelling and elaborate story with intricate and unique characters. Wield the power of an Echo and sail the Empyrean Seas.

IDEA // 07

EMPATHIZER

A short 3D mascot horror puzzle game with challenging puzzles and obstacles to navigate through, a large city-like map to roam around and understand, an intriguing story-driven narrative that entices the player to explore and discover, complex and interesting characters each with their own stories to tell, and most importantly, a protagonist that speaks through your voice.

IDEA // 08

Shadows of Hollowed Souls

A 3D souls-like action-adventure game that takes place in a steampunk world and follows the story of a resurrected being made of shadow and darkness as he tries to make sense of who he is and was. The game features a rich and appealing steampunk environment to enjoy the story and gameplay, and offers a challenging experience for those who are willing enough to take on the shadows of a hollowed soul.

IDEA // 09

Harmony of Grey

A 2.5D puzzle platformer where you play as a white cat that wakes up on a full moon after its curiosity was drawn by its transformation. The game sports a beautiful city-scape environment as a backdrop, a no-dialogue storyline, and unique, challenging puzzles that are tailored to the feline protagonist, requiring the player to not only utilize the physique of a cat as a means of getting around, but to also use the surrounding area as a resource for progression; with the end goal of finding your missing half.

IDEA // 10

The Sunset Phenomenon

A 2.5D pixel visual novel-esque adventure narrative that follows the story of a recent 12th Grade graduate, Perdido "Pedro" Gonzales, who is in a deep depression after experiencing a tragic loss when his beloved partner, Kali Lim, passed away in a fatal accident during an after-graduation celebration. The player guides Pedro as he tries to catch up with the college life he missed out. The game features a stunning retro-pixel artstyle reminiscent to its inspirations Until Then and A Space For The Unbound, but with more added variety and surprises to the gameplay that will separate it from its predecessors; carefully written and intricate characters who will accompany the player along the journey, meticulously hand-crafted world-building that inspires a more lively and immersive Filipino-based environment, and lastly, a well-crafted and emotionally driven story narrative that aims to satisfy players' crave for good and expectation-breaking plot.

IDEA // 11

Oneshot: Emergence

A modded fangame of the 2016 meta emotional, story-driven RPG game: Oneshot! In addition to more stunning, upgraded, and modern visuals that amplify the already beautiful pixel art of the original game, Emergence will feature an entirely new and unique storyline that takes place after the canonical events of the original Oneshot, our non-cat friend, Niko, a few years older; a fresh batch of original characters to complement the added story, even more detailed CGs to fill up the gallery, a more robust and slightly overhauled gameplay that stays true to the original's loop but adds more variety and surprises, a bunch of philosophical, metaphysical, and existential questions, and last but not least: a very elaborate fourth wall break.

IDEA // 12

ReBlue: Conservation

A first-person 3D science fiction conservation simulation that follows the story of a marine biologist who was given the arduous task of restoring extinct marine species through the use of a restoration machine known as "Lazarus", provided by the company they are working for. The game features a large and beautiful open ocean to dive into and explore as you search for extinction remains of marine species, a simple yet fun gameplay loop of diving, searching, restoring, conserving, and repeat; an actually elaborate lore that is spoken through hidden discoveries and environmental story-telling, encouraging the player to piece together the lore through clues and subtle exposition; and lastly, a chance to set things right.

12
IDEA // 13

Sending...

A first-person 3D cartoon-style simulation where the objective is to try and find good signal for your phone so you can send files. That's it. Legitimately that's all the gameplay is. You progress through the game by sending various files like lab reports, PowerPoint presentations, finance documents, financial reports, job reports, etc. to various people: groupmates, teachers, employers, bosses, managers, CEOs, Presidents, the United Nations, etc. and upgrade your phone to better receive more signal so you can send even more files.
