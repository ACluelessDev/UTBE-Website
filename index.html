UNTIL THE BLEEDING EDGE

You keep them running.

01

WHAT?

PROJECT // UTBE

A fast-paced and difficult 2D beat-em up game that rewards skill, reaction, awareness, timing, finesse and most importantly, the unwillingness to die.

Complete missions as a bounty hunter, mercenary, assassin, hitman, and most importantly, a son; following along the main characters' story as they do dirty jobs in hopes of saving their mother.

02

WHO?

CALLSIGN // "HAEMON" & "CREON"

A brotherly duo, Solomon and Darwin Altalune are the sons of Sola Altalune, their mother and only parent.

As mercenaries-for-hire, Solomon, callsign: "HAEMON" is the frontliner in any and all missions; skilled and professional in both hand-to-hand combat and gun-play.

Darwin, callsign: "CREON", is the brains of the operation, guiding Solomon throughout the mission and acting as his expert by providing useful briefings before the operation and tactical strategies and advice during one.

03

WHY?

CLASSIFIED // IT'S NEVER OVER

Sola Altalune, a single mother to her two sons, Solomon and Darwin, is currently suffering from cancer.

In an effort to save her in any way they can, Solomon and Darwin take up the job of mercenaries in order to raise enough money for her treatment. No matter how dirty and unjust the jobs can be.
